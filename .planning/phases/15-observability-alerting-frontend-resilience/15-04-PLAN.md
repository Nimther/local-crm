<objective>
Uniform, deeper redaction across both processes' Pino loggers (OPS-07, Pitfall 18's logging half).

Purpose: `PINO_REDACT_OPTIONS` enumerates each protected key at exactly three depths (`field`, `*.field`, `*.*.field`). `fast-redact`'s `*` matches exactly one level and there is no recursive wildcard, so a secret nested one level deeper than the enumeration is logged in plaintext. Now that `apps/worker` has a Pino logger too (plan 15-02), uniformity between the two processes becomes an assertable property rather than an aspiration.
Output: a deepened compiled path list, an extended parity test, and a new test that proves both processes' loggers behave identically.
</objective>
