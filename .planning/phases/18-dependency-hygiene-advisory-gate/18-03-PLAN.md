Fix the advisories, and let the gate built in plans 18-01/18-02 be the thing that says so.

Purpose: DEP-01 and ROADMAP SC1 — every applicable HIGH advisory in a reachable production path is
fixed by an actual upgrade. Verified live against this tree, the full-tree scan mandated by D-08
carries 8 high-severity packages holding 9 distinct HIGH advisories; every one has a non-major fix

bumped explicitly. This plan also flips the gate from RED to GREEN, which is what establishes
D-02's clean baseline: after this, any gate failure is by construction a new, untriaged finding.

Output: the upgraded lockfile and manifests, a green gate, and SPECIFICATION.md updated in the same
change per the hard project rule in `.claude/CLAUDE.md`.
