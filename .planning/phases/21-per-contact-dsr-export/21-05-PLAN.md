Add the send-related half of DSR-02 and turn the two hardest guarantees into machine-proven facts. Output: two more page readers with payload allowlisting in `dsr-export.repository.ts`, the `sends`/`sendEvents` schemas, the SC4 proof in the integration suite, and `dsr-export-isolation.test.ts`.
