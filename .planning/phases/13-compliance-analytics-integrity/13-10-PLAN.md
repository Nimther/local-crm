<objective>
Build CMP-04's synchronous half (D-01, D-04): replace `deleteContact`'s hard `DELETE FROM contacts` with an in-place anonymization that scrubs the PII columns, sets an `anonymized_at` marker, keeps the row and every foreign key pointing at it, writes an auditable erasure record, and enqueues the background scrub that plan 13-13 consumes. Mail stops in the request, not in the job.

Purpose: the erasure-versus-evidence tension resolves via anonymisation-with-retained-evidence (the ICO direction recorded in PROJECT.md). A hard delete destroys the ability to later prove a send or a suppression was lawful; keeping plaintext PII is not lawful. Anonymizing in place is what satisfies both.
Output: migration 0059 (`anonymized_at`, `erasure_records`), the rewritten `deleteContact`, the read-path changes that keep the API's delete semantics intact, and the scrub job's queue contract.
</objective>
