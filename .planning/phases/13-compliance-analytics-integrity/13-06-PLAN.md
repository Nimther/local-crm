<objective>
Complete CMP-08's recovery half (D-06, D-07): the journal from plan 13-01 records what was received, and this plan makes it replayable. A scheduled sweep finds journal rows with no ingestion-complete mark past an age threshold and re-enqueues them; an operator CLI replays an explicit time range for surgical re-runs after a bug fix misprocessed a window; a prune step keeps the horizon at days.

Purpose: post-receipt loss — a Redis flush, a worker crash, a bad deploy — becomes recoverable without the paid Email Activity API, which D-05 rejected on the same grounds Phase 11 D-05 did.
Output: a repeatable replay-sweep worker built through `@mega-crm/queue-core`, an operator range-replay script, journal pruning, and tests proving double-replay is harmless.
