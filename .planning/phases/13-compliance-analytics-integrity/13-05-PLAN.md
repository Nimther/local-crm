<objective>
Close CMP-03 (D-14): a provider event that arrives late — after the reconciler's standing 2-day window has moved past its occurrence day — currently increments the rollup row for that day but is never verified against a fresh scan, leaving an unverified band in the daily numbers. Mark such (workspace, day) rows dirty at increment time and sweep them in addition to today and yesterday.

Purpose: the phase's acceptance lens is that a daily number means exactly one thing. "Trust the increments outside the window" contradicts that; a widened blanket scan pays for it on every tick. Dirty-day marking verifies exactly the days that need it.
Output: a `dirtied_at timestamptz` column with a race-free conditional clear, the marking write on the incremental path, the dirty-day sweep on the reconciliation path, and tests covering the mid-sweep race.
</objective>
