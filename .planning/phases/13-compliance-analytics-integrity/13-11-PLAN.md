<objective>
Turn the two recorded signals into email. Plan 13-06's replay sweep and plan 13-09's reputation tick both persist state; this plan adds the two watchdogs that read that state, claim an alert slot atomically across replicas, and send — the operator for both signals (D-08, D-09), and the tenant's own workspace members for reputation (D-09), because the tenant owns the sending domain and is the only party who can act on it.

Purpose: CMP-08's ingestion health and CMP-09's reputation alert are two of the nine gaps the code-only audit missed. Both are cheap because the data is already recorded — what was missing is anyone being told.
Output: two watchdog modules following the thrice-proven `dead-letter-watchdog.ts` shape, a keyed alert claim with tier-escalation logic that no existing watchdog has, and boot wiring.
</objective>
