<objective>
Close D-16 (deferred to this phase by Phase 11 D-13): give `unknown` — and its sibling `reconciling` — an explicit, visible count in campaign stats. Today `getCampaignProgress`'s ledger re-aggregation hard-codes a four-key allow-list (`sent`, `failed`, `excluded`, `dispatching`), so a send whose true state is "we do not know whether SendGrid accepted it" is silently dropped from the ledger — the campaign's own numbers therefore do not sum to its send rows.

Purpose: the phase's acceptance lens is "numbers mean exactly what they claim". An invisible ambiguous count is the same defect class as an unverified late-event band.
Output: ambiguous counts in the progress payload and its type, a distinct UI label, ledger-sums-to-total assertions on both sides, and a refreshed send-log status-vocabulary drift assertion.
</objective>
