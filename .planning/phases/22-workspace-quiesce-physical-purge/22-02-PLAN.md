<objective>
Close the in-flight half of quiesce: a workspace soft-deleted at 12:00:00 must not send the job that was enqueued at 11:59:58. A shared fail-closed lookup runs at dispatch time on all three send paths (campaign, flow, test-send) plus the broadcast fan-out, and a refusal on the two ledgered paths becomes an honest, queryable `excluded` send fact carrying the new `workspace_deleted` reason — the same mechanism suppression exclusions already use.

The dispatch gate is the defense-in-depth layer of D-01: plan 22-04 stops the *discovery* queries from enqueueing new work, but only a dispatch-time check can kill work that was already in the queue. Two things here are not a copy of the existing gate chain: the check is per-job rather than per-contact (a workspace lookup does not belong in a per-recipient loop), and the test-send path structurally cannot use the exclusion mechanism because it never creates a `sends` row (RESEARCH Pitfall 4).

Purpose: PRG-06 / SC1 — "a soft-deleted workspace stops sending immediately" — for mail already in the pipeline.
Output: the shared quiesce helper, gates on all three dispatch paths and on the kickoff fan-out, and an integration suite that soft-deletes a workspace mid-flight and proves no mail leaves.
</objective>
