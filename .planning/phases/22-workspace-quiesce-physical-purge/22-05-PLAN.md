<objective>
Take the tracer's two-table walk to the whole tenant: every table `docs/PII-INVENTORY.md` names, in an FK order that satisfies three `ON DELETE RESTRICT` edges and never leans on a cascade, including the two large partitioned tables and the three secret tables — while the four surviving evidence sets are provably left alone. Then prove the neighbour claim that PRG-04 exists for: another workspace's rows in the same monthly partitions are byte-identical after the purge, and the purge issues no structural partition operation at all.

The FK order is not a guess and not spike territory any more: RESEARCH.md mapped the full graph (27 tenant tables all cascading from `organization`, three RESTRICT edges) and the resulting order is reproduced in the task below. The reason to delete explicitly rather than let cascades do the work is PRG-03/PRG-04, not FK correctness — a cascade fired by deleting a parent too early is an unbounded, uncheckpointed delete that the purge cannot count, resume or bound.

Purpose: PRG-02 (all PII and all secrets gone, evidence kept) and PRG-04 (batched, neighbour-safe, no structural DDL).
Output: the full table allowlist with its secret and evidence sets, the worker walking it, `docs/PII-INVENTORY.md` reconciled table-by-table, and the SC4 negative test.
</objective>
