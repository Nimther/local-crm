## Phase Goal (gap-closure slice)

**As a** marketer, **I want to** have a contact move through every step of a multi-step flow (delays, branches, sends) reliably, **so that** my triggered chain does not stall for up to 24h per step (or permanently) because a wake nudge was silently dropped.

This slice closes CR-01 (roadmap success criterion 2, FAILED in 06-VERIFICATION.md) and its compounding warning WR-08. It is engine + regression test only — no new feature surface.

<objective>
Make the flow engine's advance-nudge delivery reliable end to end.

Root cause (independently code-confirmed in 06-VERIFICATION.md): all 6 producers of `FLOW_RUN_ADVANCE_QUEUE` jobs enqueue with the deterministic `jobId: flowRunId`, while `flowRunAdvanceQueue` uses `DEFAULT_JOB_OPTIONS` that retain completed jobs 24h (`removeOnComplete: { age: 86400 }`) and failed jobs forever (`removeOnFail: false`). BullMQ `Queue.add()` no-ops while a job with that id exists in ANY state (active/completed/failed) — including the job currently being processed — so a run's next wake is silently dropped. Compounded by WR-08: the send-node and branch-node advance paths in `flow-run-advance.worker.ts` set `next_wake_at = now()` / `status = 'waiting'` but enqueue NO advance nudge at all, leaning entirely on the (also-blocked) 60s reconciliation scan.

Purpose: guarantee "a contact moves through the flow — respecting delays and branch conditions — and leaves when an exit condition is met" (FLOW-02/FLOW-03).
Output: unique-per-wake advance job ids, no-shadow queue options, send/branch forward nudges, and a real Queue/Worker integration test proving multi-step advancement.
</objective>
