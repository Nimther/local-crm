<objective>
Add time to the engine: delay/wait-until nodes with durable timers, and dispatch-time quiet-hours deferral resolved in the recipient's timezone (D-08/D-09), plus the contact/workspace timezone write-validation. Wait-until and quiet-hours math use native Intl (no date dependency). This completes FLOW-05 and the delay half of FLOW-01.

Purpose: A flow can wait, and no email is ever sent inside a contact's quiet window — deferred to the window end (the "reliability visible to the user" theme). Quiet hours are checked at dispatch time, never at schedule time (Pitfall 4).
Output: quiet-hours util + delay handler + send-handler quiet-hours gate + timezone write-validation, with unit tests including a DST case.
</objective>

> MVP user story (phase): **As a** marketer, **I want to** space out my chain's emails and never message a contact during their quiet hours, **so that** timing respects the recipient.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| client → contact/settings API | Untrusted IANA timezone string flows into Intl.DateTimeFormat at dispatch time |
| schedule time vs dispatch time | Quiet-hours settings/timezone can change between scheduling and sending |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-07-01 | Tampering / Denial of Service | invalid IANA zone crashes a worker at Intl construction | high | mitigate | validate against Intl.supportedValuesOf allowlist on write; defensively try/catch at dispatch, fall back to workspace default/UTC |
| T-06-07-02 | Repudiation | email sent inside a contact's quiet window | high | mitigate | dispatch-time isInsideQuietHours check defers the send to window end (D-14/Pitfall 4), never at schedule time |
| T-06-07-03 | Denial of Service | in-worker timer loses state on restart | medium | mitigate | next_wake_at in Postgres + delayed nudge + reconciliation backstop (06-05), no setTimeout |
| T-06-07-04 | Input Validation | timezone stored in freeform properties bypassing validation | medium | mitigate | timezone is a standard field on the allowlist, validated on every write path (API/CSV/registry) |
</threat_model>
