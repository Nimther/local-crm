- "Через полный HTTP-стек" means through the public hostname and TLS terminator, not through an in-process injection — the live leg posts to the real URL.
- "Подтверждена" for the signature requires both directions: a valid payload accepted AND a mutated payload rejected. Acceptance alone does not prove the verifier discriminates.
