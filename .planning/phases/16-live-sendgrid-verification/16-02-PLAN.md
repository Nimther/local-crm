## Planner assumptions (specless probe, no SPEC.md for this phase)

Edge-probe row **UAT-02** returned `unresolved` (English-keyed classifier, Russian REQUIREMENTS.md). Authored here as explicit planner assumptions rather than dropped:

- "Подтверждены" means each of the four event types was observed at least once, live, in this workspace — not that every send produced every type.
- Attribution correctness is asserted at three levels (send, campaign, flow step); a flow send whose step identifier is null is a failure, not a partial pass.
- The bounce is a hard bounce (a 550-class rejection). A soft bounce or a deferral does not satisfy UAT-02 and must be reported as such rather than accepted.
- Ordering between event types is not asserted — SendGrid does not guarantee it, and the platform's `occurred_at`-keyed dedup does not depend on it.
