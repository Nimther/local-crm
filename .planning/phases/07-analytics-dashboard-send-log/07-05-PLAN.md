## Phase Goal

**As a** marketer, **I want to** see end-to-end email performance — per campaign, per flow step, per contact, and across my whole workspace, down to the status of every individual message — **so that** I can tell what is working and always answer why a contact did or did not receive an email.

This plan delivers the per-message send log (ANLT-05): a complete vertical slice from a filtered read over sends through a new send-log module to the «Журнал отправок» page and its per-message drawer, built on the open/click counters from 07-01.

## Artifacts this phase produces

- `apps/api/src/modules/send-log/` module: `send-log.repository.ts` (filtered list + drawer read), `send-log.routes.ts` (`GET .../send-log`, `GET .../send-log/:sendId/events`)
- `SendLogPage` (filters, status multi-select, period presets) and `SendLogRowDrawer` (shadcn `sheet`)
- New sidebar nav item «Журнал отправок» + `/w/:slug/send-log` route

<objective>
Create the send-log module and its two endpoints — a filtered per-message list over the sends ledger (contact / campaign-or-flow / status multi-select / period) with the D-06+failed+excluded computed status, and a per-message drawer over send_events — then build the «Журнал отправок» page and its drawer, wired into the sidebar and the router with URL-param-driven filters.

Purpose: A marketer browses every message the workspace sent, filters to find the ones they care about, and clicks any row to see its full delivery chronology and reason — the "why did this message not arrive" answer (D-14). Campaign/flow/contact pages deep-link here pre-filtered (D-13).
Output: A tenant-safe send-log list + drawer API, the send-log page + sheet drawer, and sidebar/router wiring.
</objective>
