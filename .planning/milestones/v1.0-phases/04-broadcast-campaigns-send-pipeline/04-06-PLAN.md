<objective>
Wire the launch-to-send glue: the batched recipient-snapshot materialization (freeze segment membership into `campaign_recipients` at send-start, D-02), the campaign-kickoff worker (snapshot -> compute sendable_total/breakdown -> fan out one email-broadcast job per recipient), and the repeatable campaign-scheduler worker (scan due scheduled campaigns and kick them off).

Purpose: Without this plan, launching a campaign enqueues a kickoff job with no consumer. This closes the vertical slice — after this plan a launched campaign freezes its audience, fans out to the 04-04 dispatch workers, and a scheduled campaign fires when due (CAMP-02, SEND-01, the CAMP-05 denominator). It reuses the imports-csv cursor-batch loop and the shared segment engine, so there is no second membership implementation.
Output: recipient-snapshot, kickoff worker, scheduler worker, broadcast producer, registration, snapshot test.
</objective>

## Phase Goal (MVP user story)

**As a** marketer, **I want to** send a broadcast to a segment through a throttled, idempotent, suppression-aware queue, **so that** my emails reliably reach inboxes via SendGrid Dynamic Templates with per-message unsubscribe and frequency-cap compliance.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| worker -> Postgres (materialize) | Large INSERT...SELECT under a tenant's RLS scope |
| scheduler scan -> per-tenant mutation | The cross-tenant scan must re-scope RLS per campaign before writing |
| kickoff -> broadcast queue | Fan-out enqueues one job per recipient; jobIds must be idempotent |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-06-01 | Information Disclosure | cross-tenant recipient leak during scheduler scan | high | mitigate | The scheduler scans campaign metadata admin-side then re-enters withTenant(workspace_id) for every status write and every kickoff; the kickoff worker itself always re-derives workspaceId from job.data (Pitfall 5) |
| T-04-06-02 | Denial of Service | unbounded snapshot at 1M contacts holds a long transaction | high | mitigate | Batched keyset INSERT...SELECT with a scoped 60s statement_timeout and per-batch commit + resume cursor (Pattern 1 / Pitfall 3) |
| T-04-06-03 | Tampering | redelivered kickoff double-enqueues recipients | high | mitigate | Deterministic email-broadcast jobId (workspaceId-campaignId-contactId) + ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING snapshot + fan_out_complete flag; the 04-04 send insert-gate is the final backstop |
| T-04-06-04 | Tampering | membership drift between segment preview and campaign audience | medium | mitigate | Snapshot reuses compileSegmentDefinition (the SAME engine, SEGM-03), never a second evaluator |
</threat_model>
