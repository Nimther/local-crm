## Phase Goal

**As a** marketer, **I want to** watch a broadcast climb from 0 to done and reach a final "sent" state — and stop it mid-flight if needed — **so that** I can trust the campaign both completes and stays under my control.

<objective>
Close CR-05 and CR-06 (verification truths #2 and #3, CAMP-03/CAMP-05/CAMP-02).

CR-05: the ONLY code that writes `campaigns.status='sent'` is the kickoff worker's empty-audience branch (`campaign-kickoff.worker.ts:134-151`); for any non-empty audience the campaign is stuck in `sending` forever, and `sent_count`/`failed_count` are never incremented past their `DEFAULT 0`, so live progress shows `0/N` permanently.

CR-06: `send-dispatch.ts`'s campaign SELECT never reads `status`, and kickoff's fan-out checks status only once at entry — so canceling a sending campaign does not stop its already-enqueued or still-fanning-out sends.

This plan adds atomic counter increments and a completion transition (both guarded `WHERE status='sending'`) alongside every terminal send record, calls the completion check after fan-out finishes (covering the case where all sends complete before `fan_out_complete` is set), and enforces cancel at both the dispatch claim and the kickoff fan-out loop.

Purpose: make the draft→scheduled→sending→sent state machine actually reach `sent`, make progress live, and make cancel authoritative.
Output: completion/counter helpers, dispatch + kickoff wiring, and an end-to-end completion/cancel test.
</objective>
