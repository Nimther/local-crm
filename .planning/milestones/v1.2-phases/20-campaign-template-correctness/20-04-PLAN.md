## Resolved research questions

- **Why launch and schedule need no snapshot (RESEARCH Pattern 3, verified against source):** both fan out through `campaign-kickoff.worker.ts`, and both the kickoff worker and `campaign-scheduler.worker.ts` re-derive template and sender from the `campaigns` row at their own time — and they only ever act on a row whose status is `sending`/`scheduled`, which `updateCampaign` refuses to edit. The editable window a snapshot would protect does not exist for those two paths. Test-send is the exception precisely because it is enqueued from a `draft` campaign that stays editable while the job waits.
- **The override is applied inside `readSendPrereqs`, not at the `buildMailSendRequest` call site.** The function's own null check ("campaign is missing a templateId/fromEmail for dispatch") must see the EFFECTIVE values: a marketer who clears the template after queuing a test send would otherwise make the job throw even though the snapshot holds a perfectly good template. One resolution point, one check.

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-20-04-01 | Tampering | worker preferring payload-supplied template/sender | medium | mitigate | The override is consulted ONLY for `kind='test'` and only when the field is present; the campaign and flow claim paths call `readSendPrereqs` without it and keep re-deriving from the locked row. Pinned by a test asserting a snapshot field on a `kind='campaign'` job is ignored. |
| T-20-04-02 | Spoofing | a forged job injecting an arbitrary template id | low | accept | Redis is not reachable by a tenant, and the send uses the tenant's OWN decrypted SendGrid key, so a template id can only ever address a template inside that same tenant's account — there is no cross-tenant reach to gain. Accepted on the same basis the payload's existing `workspaceId`/`campaignId` fields are trusted. |
| T-20-04-03 | Denial of Service | a snapshot naming a template the tenant deleted between enqueue and dispatch | low | accept | SendGrid answers a definite 4xx, which the existing `classifyTransportError` path records as `failed` with no retry storm; the cause is the tenant's own deletion and the failure is visible in the returned outcome. |
| T-20-04-04 | Information Disclosure | logging the snapshot fields | low | mitigate | The dispatch branch's existing logging names ids and outcomes only and never the recipient address; the new fields must not be added to any log line, matching the branch's current discipline. |
| T-20-04-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | n/a | accept | No installs in this plan or phase. |
