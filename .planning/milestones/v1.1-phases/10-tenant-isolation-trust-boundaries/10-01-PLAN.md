<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** database login role. Before this phase the system assumed exactly one application login role (`mega_crm_app`); after it there are three (`mega_crm_app`, `mega_crm_scan`, `mega_crm_auth`), each with its own DSN and its own grant surface.

**Decision:** `add-alongside` — `mega_crm_app` stays the primary tenant-path identity and keeps owning every table; the two new roles are additive, own nothing, and are scoped by grants plus `TO`-clauses on policies. Promoting to a general "role registry" abstraction would be premature at three roles and would obscure the fact that each role exists for one named trust boundary.

**What would force a later promote:** a fourth role, or Phase 14's pooling work needing per-role pool configuration in more than one place. CONTEXT's deferred idea ("splitting worker/API login roles for tenant-path access") is exactly that trigger — if it lands, the ad-hoc "one env var per role" shape should become a keyed role/DSN map.

**Suggested invariant test:** `scan.test.ts` asserts, for every non-owner role, that it is `NOBYPASSRLS` and owns zero tables — a rule written once that holds for role four as well.
</assumption_delta_decision>
