<objective>
Extend the scan role from the tracer's single table to every table a cross-workspace consumer actually reads, and move the three remaining worker-level scan consumers onto `withCrossWorkspaceScan`.

Purpose: SEC-01/SEC-02 require all cross-tenant discovery to run under the dedicated role. This plan also closes Pitfall 3 — two of the four legacy scan policies (`flow_runs_due_scan`, `flows_segment_sweep_scan`) have no predicate beyond the marker GUC, so simply re-scoping them by role would hand the scan role every row in `flow_runs` and `flows` unconditionally.
Output: migration 0042 (grants + narrowed role-scoped policies for `flow_runs`, `flows`, `contacts`, `sends`, `organization`), three migrated consumers, and predicate-narrowing assertions in `scan.test.ts`.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| worker scan pool → Postgres | The cross-tenant read surface; every additional grant widens it |
| scan-role policy predicates → row visibility | The only thing limiting which rows a compromised worker can read |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-03-01 | Information Disclosure | `flow_runs_scan` / `flows_scan` predicates | high | mitigate | Narrowing predicates restored from the consumers' own WHERE clauses; asserted by tests that seed rows the predicate must exclude |
| T-10-03-02 | Information Disclosure | `contacts_scan` / `sends_scan` unrestricted rows | medium | accept | Unavoidable for FK re-validation and unpredictable `send_id` resolution; bounded by SELECT-only grants, two known readers, and plan 10-08's payload-free assertion. Recorded as RESEARCH.md Assumption A3 |
| T-10-03-03 | Elevation of Privilege | scan-role write access | high | mitigate | Only `GRANT SELECT` is issued; an acceptance criterion greps the migration for any write grant |
| T-10-03-04 | Information Disclosure | unscoped app policies leaking into scan query plans | high | mitigate | Four `ALTER POLICY ... TO mega_crm_app` statements, per Pitfall 2 and the migration-0019 precedent |
| T-10-03-05 | Elevation of Privilege | scan role reaching ungranted tables | medium | mitigate | Test 5 asserts a read of `flow_versions` rejects — grants are the enumerated allowlist, not a default |
| T-10-03-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | accept | No package-manager installs in this plan (RESEARCH.md § Package Legitimacy Audit) |
</threat_model>
