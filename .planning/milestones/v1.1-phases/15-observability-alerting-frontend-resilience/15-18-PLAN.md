<objective>
OPS-15: a runbook per alert, and the architecture record of how observability actually works here.

Purpose: an alert without a recovery procedure is a notification, not an operational control. This phase added four in-app alerts and two cloud backstop rules; each needs a document that tells the operator what it means, how to confirm it, what to do, and how to tune it. The threshold values this phase chose are first estimates by explicit design, so "how to tune" is not optional content.
Output: six runbooks, a coverage check that keeps them in step with the alerts, and the ARCHITECTURE.md observability sections.
</objective>
