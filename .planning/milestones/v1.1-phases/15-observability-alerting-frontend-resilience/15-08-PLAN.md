<objective>
OPS-06: every BullMQ job in `apps/worker` is instrumented once, through one helper, and no raw `console.*` remains.

Purpose: the worker is the process that does the sending, and it currently logs through `console.*` with no structured fields, no redaction and no correlation. Plan 15-02 gave it a Pino logger and wired one queue; this plan rolls the instrumentation across all of them through a single shared wrapper, so hosted logs, Sentry capture and trace correlation all attach at exactly one place instead of twenty.
Output: `wrapProcessor`, applied at every `create*Worker` factory, with a coverage test that fails when a new factory bypasses it.

Note on counts: the ROADMAP says "13 `create*Worker` processors" — that number is stale. `apps/worker/src/server.ts` builds 20 factories today. The acceptance criterion below is structural (every factory, enumerated from source) rather than a fixed count, so it cannot go stale again.
</objective>
