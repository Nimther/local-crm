<objective>
OPS-13, first two alerts: queue depth and oldest job age.

Purpose: the send pipeline's two most direct "is it moving" signals. Queue depth catches a lane that is filling faster than it drains; oldest job age catches a lane that has stopped draining at all, and — per the roadmap's explicit lock — also the age of the oldest send still awaiting delivery evidence in `reconciling_since`.
Output: two watchdogs following the proven `send-reconciler-watchdog` shape, on the shared claim primitive from plan 15-12.

A note on the roadmap's wording: it says the oldest-job-age and webhook-lag alerts "query `reconciling_since` directly". A send sitting in `reconciling` has no pending BullMQ job, so reading only `reconciling_since` would leave a genuinely stalled queue completely unmonitored. This plan therefore evaluates BOTH signals inside the oldest-job-age watchdog — the oldest pending BullMQ job AND the oldest unresolved `reconciling_since` — which honours the lock and closes the gap it would otherwise leave.
</objective>
